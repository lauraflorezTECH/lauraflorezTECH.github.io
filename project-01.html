01 / PROJECT OVERVIEW

The business challenge

Inconsistent transaction records can make sales reports unreliable. This project focused on preparing sales data for reporting and exploring sales performance across products, locations and time periods.

02 / PROCESS

How I approached the analysis

STEP 01

Assess data quality

Checked missing values, duplicate order IDs and inconsistent fields. The project report noted 10 missing unit prices and 16 missing transaction totals.

STEP 02

Clean & structure

Standardized city names and dates, and separated product details into attributes suitable for analysis.

STEP 03

Report & communicate

Created summary indicators, analyzed sales by city and product, and prepared an executive report.

04 / BUSINESS RECOMMENDATION

Turning data into decisions

Compare sales patterns across cities and product categories to identify opportunities for targeted marketing. Investigate weaker-performing segments and validate the underlying data before allocating additional budget.
