Walmart Sales & Department Performance

Transforming weekly store sales into department-level KPIs, a dashboard and executive recommendations.

01 / BUSINESS QUESTION

Which departments drive sales performance?

The project analyzed 2012 weekly sales by Walmart store and department to understand sales efficiency per square meter and each department's share of total sales. The goal was to translate raw transaction data into decisions about inventory and commercial priorities.

02 / ANALYTICAL PROCESS

From raw data to business insights

STEP 01 · DATA PREPARATION

Clean and join data

Combined weekly sales records with store and department reference tables, standardized dates and prepared a clean analytical dataset.

STEP 02 · METRICS

Calculate performance KPIs

Measured sales per square meter and department share of total sales to compare efficiency and contribution.

STEP 03 · REPORTING

Build executive reporting

Used pivot tables, dashboard summaries and a Context–Finding–Implication structure to communicate business recommendations.

04 / DATA QUALITY & RECOMMENDATIONS

Make reporting more reliable

The workbook's QA checklist flags 6,435 records without a mapped department and 27 negative sales values, while its store-size check reports no zero or missing store sizes. These checks indicate areas to review before using the figures for high-stakes decisions.

Business implications

Prioritize stock availability in departments with high contribution and sales efficiency. Investigate departments with lower contribution through targeted merchandising or marketing tests, while resolving department mapping gaps and validating negative sales records.
